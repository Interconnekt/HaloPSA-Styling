Accessing the Aryza-Hosted Azure Virtual Desktop

Tags  ·  ida, aryza Views 1  ·  Helpful 0
Description
FieldDetails
ClientIDA
CategoryRemote Access, Aryza Azure Virtual Desktop environment

Who this is for. IDA staff who have been invited as a guest user into the Aryza-hosted Microsoft Entra ID tenant and need to reach the IDA Desktop virtual desktop using their existing @ida.com.au account.

1. How this works

Your everyday IDA account lives in IDA's own Microsoft tenant. The virtual desktop you need to access Aryza is hosted by Aryza in a separate Microsoft tenant. That second tenant is also named IDA, so the only reliable way to tell the two apart is by its domain: idainvestment.onmicrosoft.com.

Aryza has invited your IDA account into that tenant as a guest, which means you sign in with your existing @ida.com.au account. There is no second username or password to remember.

Accepting the invitation does not give Aryza access to your IDA mailbox, files or data. It only allows your IDA account to sign in to ARMnet.

Because your account now belongs to two organisations, the Windows App has to be told which organisation to look in. By default it opens your home IDA tenant, which has no virtual desktops published, so you will see a "no resources" message until you switch across to the Aryza-hosted tenant. Switching organisation is the single most important step in this guide.

2. Accepting your guest invitation

Nothing else in this guide will work until the invitation is accepted. You only do this once.

Step 1: Open the invitation email and accept it

  1. In your IDA mailbox, find the email titled "IDA invited you to access applications within their organization", sent by Microsoft Invitations on behalf of IDA (MSSecurity-noreply@microsoft.com) and copied to your Aryza contact.
  2. Outlook flags it as external. This is expected, as Microsoft sends the invitation on Aryza's behalf.
  3. Check the body of the email shows Organization: IDA and Domain: idainvestment.onmicrosoft.com.
  4. Click Accept invitation.

The invitation email. Confirm the organisation and domain, then click Accept invitation.

Step 2: Review the permissions and accept

  1. Sign in with your @ida.com.au account if you are prompted.
  2. Microsoft shows Permissions requested by: IDA, listing what the organisation is allowed to do: receive your profile data, collect and log your activity, and use that profile and activity data.
  3. Check the domain reads idainvestment.onmicrosoft.com, then click Accept.

This is normal and does not mean anything is broken. The web client has opened your home IDA tenant, which has no virtual desktops published. The Aryza desktop lives in the other tenant, so the next three steps switch you across.

Switching organisation is the single most important step in this guide.

3. Connecting from a terminal

For administrators, the session host can also be checked from PowerShell:

Get-AzWvdSessionHost -ResourceGroupName "rg-armnet-prod" `
    -HostPoolName "hp-ida-desktop" | Select-Object Name, Status

5. Troubleshooting

SymptomWhat to do
"Your system administrator hasn't set up any resources"Expected You are in your home IDA tenant. Follow Steps 6 to 8 and switch to the Aryza-hosted tenant.
No second organisation appears in the Organizations panelSelf-fix Your guest invitation has not been accepted yet. Go back to section 2 and complete Steps 1 and 2, then sign out and back in.
IDA Desktop is missing after switching organisationSupport You are in the right tenant but the desktop has not been assigned to you. Contact the Aryza service desk and ask to be added to the IDA ARMnet application group.
Sign-in loops or fails repeatedlySelf-fix Try an InPrivate / Incognito browser window, or clear cookies for microsoft.com. Cached credentials from another Microsoft account are the usual cause.

Still stuck?

Log a ticket with support@interconnekt.com.au if you cannot sign in with your ida.com.au account at all, or with the Aryza service desk if you can sign in and switch organisation but the IDA Desktop resource is missing. Include a screenshot of what you see and the email address you signed in with.
